I have rebuilt my two [competition projects]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) and updated their repositories, reports and posters.
The ECG project now starts from the raw MIT-BIH records so that patients can be kept apart between training and test sets; separating them lowers macro F1 from 0.963 to 0.592.
The EEG project corrects errors in the 2025 version and compares decoders under cross-session, leave-one-subject-out and transfer protocols, with three comparisons fixed in an analysis plan before the runs.
The Research page also lists two applied machine-learning projects from Microsoft AI School.
